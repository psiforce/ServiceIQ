Best Thermostat Settings for Comfort and Savings

  • Summer: 78°F when you're home, a few degrees warmer when away
  • Winter: 68°F when you're home, a few degrees cooler when asleep or away
  • Set the fan to Auto rather than On for lower humidity and energy use in summer
  • Avoid big setbacks with heat pumps; large jumps can trigger expensive backup electric heat
