Seasonal HVAC Checklist

Spring

  • Schedule an AC tune-up ($129, included with Comfort Club)
  • Clear leaves and debris from around the outdoor unit; keep 2 feet of clearance
  • Replace the air filter
  • Test cooling before the first hot day

Fall

  • Schedule a furnace tune-up ($129, included with Comfort Club)
  • Test your carbon monoxide and smoke alarms and replace batteries
  • Replace the air filter and humidifier pad
  • Make sure nothing is stored against the furnace or water heater
