Heat Pump Repair, Maintenance & Installation

A heat pump heats and cools your home with one outdoor unit by moving heat instead of burning fuel. We repair all major heat pump brands and install Aerotherm AH18 series heat pumps and Northline cold-climate heat pumps.

Dual fuel option

Many Maple Falls homeowners pair a heat pump with a gas furnace ("dual fuel"). The heat pump handles mild weather and the furnace takes over in very cold temperatures.

Typical investment

Heat pump system replacements typically range from $7,900 to $15,500. An exact price requires a free in-home estimate and written quote. Heat pump tune-ups are $149 and cover both heating and cooling modes.

Installation usually takes 1-2 days.
