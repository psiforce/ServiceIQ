About Cedar Ridge Comfort Systems

Cedar Ridge Comfort Systems has been a family-owned heating and cooling company in Maple Falls since 2004. We started with one truck and now employ 18 service technicians, 6 installation crews and a local office team.

  • $2 million general liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage
