Ductless Mini-Split Systems

Ductless mini-splits are ideal for additions, sunrooms, garages converted to living space, and older homes without ductwork. We install Northline ductless systems and service Mitsubishi Electric, Fujitsu, Daikin and other major ductless brands.

  • Single-zone systems typically range from $3,900 to $6,500 installed
  • Multi-zone systems are quoted after an in-home assessment
  • Installation takes about one day per one or two zones
  • Indoor heads should have their filters rinsed monthly during heavy use
