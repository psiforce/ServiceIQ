Carbon Monoxide Safety at Home

Carbon monoxide (CO) is an invisible, odorless gas produced when fuels burn. A damaged furnace heat exchanger, blocked flue or poorly vented appliance can let CO into your home.

Symptoms of CO exposure

Headache, dizziness, nausea, fatigue and confusion, often affecting several people or pets in the home at once and improving when you leave.

Protect your family

  • Install CO alarms on every level of the home and near sleeping areas
  • Test alarms monthly and replace batteries every fall
  • Have fuel-burning heating equipment inspected every year; our furnace tune-up includes a CO check
  • Never run a generator, grill or car in an attached garage or indoors

If your CO alarm sounds

Get everyone outside to fresh air immediately and call 911. Do not go back inside until emergency responders say it is safe. Then call us at (555) 014-2299 to inspect your heating equipment.
