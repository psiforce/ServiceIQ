SEER2 Ratings Explained

SEER2 measures how efficiently an air conditioner or heat pump cools over a season. Since January 1, 2023, efficiency ratings use the SEER2 test method, which better reflects real-world duct conditions. SEER2 numbers are slightly lower than the old SEER numbers for the same equipment.

What it means for you

  • Higher SEER2 means lower cooling costs, but a higher upfront price.
  • In northern states such as Ohio, the federal minimum for new central AC is 13.4 SEER2.
  • Our Aerotherm AT16 series ACs are rated up to 16.2 SEER2.
  • Variable-speed systems (17+ SEER2) also improve humidity control and comfort.
