How Often Should You Change Your Air Filter?

A clean filter protects your equipment, keeps air flowing and lowers energy bills. A clogged filter is one of the most common reasons we see frozen AC coils and furnaces that overheat and shut off.

General guidelines

  • 1-inch filters: check monthly, replace every 1-3 months
  • 4-5 inch media filters: replace every 6-12 months
  • Replace more often with pets, allergies, smokers in the home or construction dust

Which MERV rating?

MERV 8-11 filters work well for most homes. Very high MERV filters (13+) can restrict airflow in systems that weren't designed for them. Ask your technician before upgrading.

How to change it

  1. Turn the system off at the thermostat.
  2. Slide out the old filter and note the size printed on its frame.
  3. Insert the new filter with the airflow arrow pointing toward the furnace or air handler.
  4. Turn the system back on.
