AC Not Cooling? Check These Things Before You Call

These safe homeowner checks can solve simple problems. If they don't, stop and call a professional.

  1. Thermostat: Set to Cool, set below the current room temperature, fan on Auto. Replace thermostat batteries if the display is blank.
  2. Air filter: Replace a dirty filter.
  3. Breakers: Check the AC and furnace/air handler breakers. You may reset a tripped breaker once. If it trips again, leave it off and call us.
  4. Outdoor unit: Make sure it is running and clear of leaves, grass and debris (keep 2 feet of clearance).
  5. Vents: Make sure supply and return vents are open and unblocked.

Stop and call a technician if

  • You see ice on the refrigerant lines or indoor coil (turn the system off first)
  • The outdoor fan isn't spinning or you hear buzzing
  • A breaker trips repeatedly
  • You smell burning

Never open electrical panels on the unit or try to add refrigerant yourself. Refrigerant work requires EPA certification.
