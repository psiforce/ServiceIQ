Why Do HVAC Companies Charge a Diagnostic Fee?

A diagnostic fee covers the technician's time, travel and testing equipment needed to find the real cause of a problem, not just the symptom.

At Cedar Ridge, our diagnostic visit is just $79, and it is waived when you approve the repair during the same visit. Comfort Club members never pay a diagnostic fee during standard hours.

Be cautious of "free" service calls; the cost is usually built into higher repair prices.
