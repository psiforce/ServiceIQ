Why Is There Ice on My AC?

Ice on the indoor coil or the large copper refrigerant line usually means low airflow (dirty filter, blocked vents, failing blower) or low refrigerant from a leak.

What to do

  1. Turn the thermostat from Cool to Off.
  2. Set the fan to On so warm indoor air helps melt the ice. Thawing usually takes 2-4 hours.
  3. Replace the air filter if it is dirty.
  4. Put towels near the indoor unit to catch melt water.
  5. Schedule a service visit. If ice returns, the cause needs to be diagnosed.

Never chip or scrape ice off the coil; you can puncture it. If a refrigerant leak is suspected, a leak search is $295 and refrigerant is charged per pound.
