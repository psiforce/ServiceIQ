Air Conditioner Repair

We repair central air conditioners and heat pumps of all major brands, including Carrier, Trane, Lennox, Rheem, Goodman, Daikin and more. Our technicians carry common parts on every truck so most repairs are finished on the first visit.

Common AC problems we fix

  • AC running but blowing warm air
  • Outdoor unit not turning on (often a failed capacitor or contactor)
  • Frozen evaporator coil or ice on refrigerant lines
  • Water leaking from the indoor unit (clogged condensate drain)
  • Refrigerant leaks
  • Noisy fan motors

How AC repair works

  1. Your technician diagnoses the problem. The standard-hours diagnostic is $89.
  2. You receive an upfront, flat-rate price before any work begins.
  3. If you approve the repair during the same visit, the diagnostic fee is waived.
  4. Repairs carry our 2-year labor warranty.

We do not repair window or portable air conditioners.
