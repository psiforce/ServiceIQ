Furnace Not Heating? Safe Checks Before You Call

If you smell gas, leave the home immediately and call your gas utility or 911 from outside. Do not do any of the checks below.

  1. Thermostat: Set to Heat and above the room temperature. Replace batteries if the display is blank.
  2. Air filter: A badly clogged filter can make the furnace overheat and shut itself off.
  3. Furnace power switch: Furnaces have a switch that looks like a light switch, usually near the unit. Make sure it is on.
  4. Furnace door: The front panel must be fully closed. A safety switch stops the furnace if it is loose.
  5. Breaker: Check the furnace breaker. Reset once only.
  6. Error code: Many furnaces have a blinking light. Count the blinks and tell us the pattern when you call.

Do not adjust gas valves, remove burners, or try to clean or replace the flame sensor or igniter yourself. These repairs require a trained technician.
