24/7 Emergency Service

If you smell gas or your carbon monoxide alarm is sounding, leave the home now and call 911 or your gas utility from outside. Do not call us first.

When to call our emergency line: (555) 014-2299

  • No heat when it is below 40°F outside
  • No cooling when it is above 90°F outside, especially with elderly residents, infants or medical needs
  • Water leaking from your HVAC equipment
  • Burning smell from your system (turn the system off first; if there is smoke or fire, call 911)

After-hours pricing

After-hours, weekend and holiday diagnostics are $169, or $99 for Comfort Club members. After-hours means before 7:00 AM or after 6:00 PM on weekdays, Saturdays after 2:00 PM, Sundays and company holidays.

We do not respond to active gas leaks until the gas utility has made the home safe.
