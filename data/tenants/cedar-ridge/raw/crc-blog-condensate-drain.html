Water Leaking From Your AC? It's Probably the Condensate Drain

Your AC removes moisture from the air. That water drains through a PVC condensate line. Algae and sludge can clog it and cause water to back up and leak.

Prevention

  • Pour one cup of plain white vinegar into the drain line access tee (if your system has one) every three months during cooling season.
  • Premium Comfort Club members get an annual drain line treatment.

If it is leaking now

Turn the system off at the thermostat and move belongings away from the water. Many systems have a float switch that shuts the AC off when the pan fills; this is a safety feature. Schedule a service visit. Condensate drain line clearing is $149.
