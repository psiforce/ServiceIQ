Gas Furnace Installation & Replacement

We install high-efficiency Aerotherm gas furnaces, including the AG96 series (96% AFUE) with variable-speed blowers. Every installation includes a load calculation, a combustion safety test and carbon monoxide testing.

Typical investment

Gas furnace replacements typically range from $4,200 to $8,900 depending on efficiency, venting and ductwork changes. A free in-home estimate is required for an exact written quote.

What's included

  • Permits, inspection and code-required venting updates
  • Removal and disposal of the old furnace
  • Manufacturer warranty registration within 30 days
  • 10-Year Labor Guarantee with annual maintenance (see Warranty Policy)

Most furnace replacements are completed in one day.
