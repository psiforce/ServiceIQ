Furnace Repair

We repair natural gas and propane forced-air furnaces of all major brands. We do not service oil furnaces, boilers or geothermal systems.

Common furnace problems

  • No heat or lukewarm air
  • Furnace starts then shuts off (often a dirty flame sensor)
  • Igniter failure
  • Blower motor problems or loud noises
  • Blinking error codes on the control board

Safety first

If you smell gas, leave the home and call your gas utility or 911 from outside. If your carbon monoxide alarm sounds, get everyone outside and call 911. Do not attempt to repair gas valves, burners or wiring yourself.
