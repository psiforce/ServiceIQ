Air Conditioner Installation & Replacement

Cedar Ridge is an authorized dealer for Aerotherm central air conditioners. We size every system with a room-by-room load calculation rather than simply matching the size of your old unit.

What's included

  • Free in-home estimate (60-90 minutes) and written quote valid for 30 days
  • Removal and responsible disposal of old equipment and refrigerant
  • New line set or line set flush as needed, new pad and disconnect
  • Permits and inspection scheduling
  • Manufacturer warranty registration on your behalf within 30 days
  • 10-Year Labor Guarantee (conditions apply; see our Warranty Policy)

Typical investment

Central AC replacements (2-5 ton) typically range from $5,800 to $11,500 depending on size, efficiency and installation requirements. Your exact price is provided in a written quote after the in-home assessment. Financing is available on approved credit.

Most AC replacements are completed in one day.
