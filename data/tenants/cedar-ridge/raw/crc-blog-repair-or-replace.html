Repair or Replace? How to Decide

There's no single rule, but these factors help:

  • Age: Central ACs and heat pumps typically last 12-15 years; gas furnaces 15-20 years.
  • The $5,000 rule of thumb: Multiply the equipment's age by the repair cost. If the result is over $5,000, replacement is often the better value.
  • Refrigerant: R-22 systems with leaks are usually better replaced.
  • Frequency: Multiple repairs in two years suggests the system is near the end of its life.
  • Comfort and bills: Uneven temperatures and rising energy bills are signs too.
