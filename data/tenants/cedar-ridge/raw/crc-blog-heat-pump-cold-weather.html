Do Heat Pumps Work in Cold Weather?

Yes. Modern cold-climate heat pumps, such as the Northline cold-climate series we install, keep heating efficiently down to about 5°F. Below that, backup heat (electric heat strips or a gas furnace in a dual-fuel system) helps maintain comfort.

What's normal in winter

  • Steam or vapor from the outdoor unit during defrost cycles
  • A whooshing sound when the unit switches into or out of defrost
  • Air from vents that feels warm but not hot (heat pump air is gentler than furnace air)

When to call us

  • The outdoor unit is completely encased in ice
  • The "Aux" or "Emergency heat" indicator stays on constantly
  • The home cannot reach the set temperature
