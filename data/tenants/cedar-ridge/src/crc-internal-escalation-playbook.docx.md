# Escalation Playbook (INTERNAL)

_Internal use only · access_scope: internal · Effective January 1, 2026 · Fictional company for ServiceIQ testing_

| Priority | Situations | Who responds | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Gas smell (after utility clears), CO alarm (after 911 clears), burning smell, no heat below 40°F with vulnerable occupants | On-call technician via emergency line | Call back within 15 minutes |
| P2 | Binding quote request, warranty determination, technician needed, complaints | CSR, then service manager | Same business day |
| P3 | Unanswered website questions, knowledge gaps, general callbacks | CSR | Next business day |

## Handoff checklist

1. Read the chat transcript and the assistant's cited sources before calling.
2. Confirm name, address, phone and the best time to call.
3. For warranty questions, pull the invoice and the equipment registration.
4. Log the outcome in the CRM, and flag any missing or wrong knowledge article to the Knowledge Administrator.
