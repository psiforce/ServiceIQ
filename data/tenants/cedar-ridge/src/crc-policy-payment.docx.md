# Payment Policy

_Version 2 · Effective January 1, 2026 · Fictional company for ServiceIQ testing_

## Accepted payment methods

- Cash
- Personal checks (returned checks incur a $35 fee)
- Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover
- ACH bank transfer

We do not accept cryptocurrency or payment apps for in-home payments.

## When payment is due

- **Repairs and maintenance:** due when the work is completed.
- **Installations:** a 10% deposit is due when the contract is signed, and the balance is due when the installation is completed.
- **Commercial accounts:** approved commercial customers may be invoiced with net-30 terms.

## Other details

- Comfort Club monthly and annual dues are charged automatically to the card or bank account on file.
- Invoices are emailed after each visit.
- Financing for installations is available through our lending partner; see the Financing Terms.
