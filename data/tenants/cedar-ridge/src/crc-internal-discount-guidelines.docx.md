# Discount & Courtesy Credit Guidelines (INTERNAL)

_Internal use only · access_scope: internal · Not for customer disclosure · Effective January 1, 2026 · Fictional company for ServiceIQ testing_

- CSRs may offer a courtesy credit of up to **10%** on a repair invoice for service-recovery situations, with manager approval.
- Service managers may approve up to **20%** or waive a diagnostic fee for repeat-visit complaints.
- Installation price matching against a written competitor quote for equivalent equipment requires general manager approval.
- Senior (65+) and military discount: 5% off repairs, not combinable with Comfort Club discounts. **This discount is only offered when the customer asks; it is not advertised.**
- Never promise a discount in writing before approval is recorded in the CRM.
