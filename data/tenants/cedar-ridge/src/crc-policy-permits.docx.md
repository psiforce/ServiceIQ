# Permits & Inspections Policy

_Effective January 1, 2026 · Fictional company for ServiceIQ testing_

- Cedar Ridge obtains all permits required by the city or county for equipment installations and replacements.
- Permit fees charged by the city or county are passed through to the customer at cost. A **$45 permit administration fee** applies per permit.
- We schedule the final inspection and attend it when the inspector requires a contractor to be present. The homeowner or an authorized adult must provide access.
- Repairs generally do not require a permit. Gas line changes, new circuits and venting changes may require one; the technician will explain if a permit is needed.
- We will not perform work that requires a permit without obtaining one.
