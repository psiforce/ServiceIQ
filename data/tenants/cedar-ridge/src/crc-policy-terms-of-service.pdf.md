# Terms of Service

_Version 4 · Effective January 1, 2026 · Fictional company for ServiceIQ testing_

## 1. Estimates and pricing

Cedar Ridge uses upfront, flat-rate pricing. After diagnosis, the technician provides a written price for the recommended work. No work begins without the customer's approval. Prices shared by phone, website or chat are published prices or typical ranges and are **not binding quotes**. A binding price is only provided in writing by a Cedar Ridge technician or comfort advisor.

## 2. Authorization

An adult aged 18 or older who owns the property or is authorized by the owner must be present to approve work. Tenants must have the property owner's permission for installations.

## 3. Access and safety

The customer must provide safe, clear access to equipment. Technicians may decline to work in unsafe conditions, including active gas leaks, standing water around electrical equipment, aggressive animals, or attics above safe temperatures.

## 4. Hidden conditions

If unforeseen conditions (such as asbestos, mold, code violations or damaged ductwork) are discovered, work will stop and the customer will receive a revised price before work continues. Cedar Ridge does not remove asbestos.

## 5. Payment

Payment terms are described in the Payment Policy.

## 6. Limitation of liability

Cedar Ridge is not responsible for pre-existing conditions or for damage resulting from equipment failure unrelated to our work. Warranty terms are described in the Warranty Policy.

## 7. Communications

By booking service, the customer agrees to receive appointment reminders and arrival texts. Customers may opt out of marketing messages at any time.
