# Aerotherm AH18 Series Heat Pump: Homeowner's Manual (Excerpt)

_Aerotherm is a fictional manufacturer created for ServiceIQ testing · Document AH18-HO-2025 rev A_

## Models covered

AH18HP024, AH18HP036, AH18HP048 and AH18HP060 variable-speed heat pumps (up to 18.0 SEER2, 9.0 HSPF2), R-454B refrigerant.

## Normal winter operation

- **Defrost cycle:** In cold, damp weather frost builds up on the outdoor coil. The unit automatically runs a defrost cycle for 2-10 minutes. During defrost the outdoor fan stops, you may see steam rising from the unit and hear a whoosh when the reversing valve shifts. This is normal.
- **Auxiliary heat:** When the heat pump alone cannot keep up, the thermostat turns on backup heat and may display "Aux Heat". Brief periods of auxiliary heat during cold weather or recovery from a setback are normal.
- **Emergency heat:** "Em Heat" runs only the backup heat source and should be used only if the heat pump has failed, until service arrives. It is more expensive to operate.

## When to call for service

- The outdoor unit is completely covered in ice and does not clear after a defrost cycle
- Auxiliary heat runs continuously in mild weather
- The unit makes grinding or loud banging noises

## Homeowner maintenance

- Keep 24 inches of clearance around the outdoor unit and keep it raised above snow level.
- Never pour hot water on the outdoor coil or chip ice from it.
- Replace indoor air filters regularly. Have the system serviced twice a year (spring and fall).

## Warranty

10-year limited parts warranty when registered within 60 days of installation; 5-year limited parts warranty if not registered. Labor is not covered by the manufacturer.
