# Comfort Club Membership Terms

_Version 2 · Effective January 1, 2026 · Fictional company for ServiceIQ testing_

## 1. Plans and pricing

| Plan | Monthly | Annual | Systems covered |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | $16.95 | $189 | 1 cooling system and 1 heating system at one address |
| Premium | $24.95 | $279 | Up to 2 cooling systems and 2 heating systems at one address |

A heat pump counts as one cooling system and one heating system.

## 2. Benefits

- One cooling tune-up (spring) and one heating tune-up (fall) per covered system each membership year
- No diagnostic fee during standard hours; after-hours diagnostics are $99
- Repair discount: 15% (Basic) or 20% (Premium). Discounts do not apply to new equipment installations, which receive member pricing on the written quote.
- 10% off duct cleaning and indoor air quality products
- Priority scheduling ahead of non-member service calls of the same urgency
- One missed appointment fee waiver per membership year
- Premium only: annual condensate drain line treatment and a 3-year labor warranty on repairs made during membership

## 3. Term and renewal

Memberships begin on the enrollment date and renew automatically each month (monthly plans) or each year (annual plans) using the payment method on file. We send a renewal reminder 30 days before annual renewals.

## 4. Cancellation and refunds

- Monthly plans: cancel anytime with 30 days notice; no refund for the current month.
- Annual plans: cancel anytime. You receive a prorated refund for unused months minus the regular price of services already used during the membership year (for example, a $129 tune-up).
- Benefits end on the cancellation date.

## 5. Limitations

Unused tune-ups do not roll over to the next membership year. Membership is tied to the service address and is not transferable to another address. Membership does not guarantee a specific appointment time.
