# CSR Response Templates (INTERNAL)

_Internal use only · access_scope: internal · Effective January 1, 2026 · Fictional company for ServiceIQ testing_

These approved templates are used by customer service representatives and by the website assistant for fallback and escalation messages.

## Cannot confirm (fallback)

"I don't have confirmed information about that in our approved resources, so I don't want to guess. A member of our team can help. Would you like us to contact you, or you can call (555) 014-2200 during business hours."

## Binding quote request

"Our published prices and typical ranges are a starting point, but an exact price requires a diagnosis by a technician or a free in-home estimate. Would you like us to set that up?"

## Warranty question

"Coverage depends on your equipment, install date, registration and the work performed. Our office can check your coverage with your invoice number or the model and serial number."

## Availability request

"I can't see our live schedule here. Our team can find the next available arrival window for you. Would you like a callback?"

## Emergency

"If you smell gas or your CO alarm is sounding, please leave the home now and call 911 or your gas utility from outside. Once everyone is safe, our 24/7 emergency line is (555) 014-2299."
