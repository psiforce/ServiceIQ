# Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy

_Version 2 · Effective January 1, 2026 · Fictional company for ServiceIQ testing_

## Service appointments

- Cancel or reschedule **free of charge with at least 24 hours notice** by calling (555) 014-2200.
- Cancellations with less than 24 hours notice, or visits where the technician cannot access the equipment, may be charged a **$49 missed appointment fee**.
- Comfort Club members receive one missed appointment fee waiver per membership year.
- If Cedar Ridge must reschedule because of weather, emergencies or technician illness, there is no fee and we will offer the next available arrival window.

## Installation contracts

- You may cancel a signed installation contract **within 3 business days** of signing for a full refund of the deposit.
- After 3 business days, if equipment has already been ordered, a **restocking fee of 15% of the equipment cost** applies.
- Custom-ordered equipment, once delivered, is non-refundable.
- Permit fees already paid to the city or county are non-refundable.
