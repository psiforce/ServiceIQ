# Refrigerant Service Policy

_Effective January 1, 2026 · Fictional company for ServiceIQ testing_

- All refrigerant work is performed by EPA Section 608 certified technicians.
- Refrigerant is charged by the pound added: R-410A at $95 per pound and R-454B at $125 per pound.
- **R-22 recharges are not offered.** For R-22 systems, we diagnose the problem and offer a free replacement consultation.
- If a system needs more than 2 pounds of refrigerant, or needs refrigerant added more than once in 12 months, we recommend a leak search ($295) before adding more. Repeatedly topping off a leaking system wastes money and harms the environment.
- Recovered refrigerant from replaced equipment is recycled or disposed of according to EPA rules.
- We do not sell refrigerant to customers or uncertified contractors.
