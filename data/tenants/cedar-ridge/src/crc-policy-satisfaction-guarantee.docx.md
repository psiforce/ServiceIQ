# 30-Day Satisfaction Guarantee

_Effective January 1, 2026 · Fictional company for ServiceIQ testing_

If you are not satisfied with a repair or maintenance visit, tell us within **30 days** of the service date. We will send a technician back to make it right at no additional charge.

- The guarantee covers the quality of our work and the technician's service.
- It does not cover unrelated new problems, damage excluded by the Warranty Policy, or problems caused by changes made by others after our visit.
- The guarantee does not provide cash refunds for completed repairs. A service manager reviews any refund request individually.
- For new system installations, a comfort advisor will follow up within 7 days to make sure the system meets your expectations.
