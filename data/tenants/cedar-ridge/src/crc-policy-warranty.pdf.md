# Cedar Ridge Comfort Systems: Warranty Policy

_Version 3 · Effective January 1, 2026 · Supersedes all earlier warranty statements, including website text dated 2023 · Fictional company for ServiceIQ testing_

## 1. Repair labor warranty

All repairs performed by Cedar Ridge carry a **2-year labor warranty** from the date of service. If the same repair fails because of our workmanship within 2 years, we will correct it at no labor charge.

Premium Comfort Club members receive a **3-year labor warranty** on repairs performed while their membership is active.

## 2. Parts used in repairs

- Parts we supply are covered by the manufacturer's parts warranty.
- If the manufacturer's parts warranty is shorter than 1 year, Cedar Ridge guarantees parts we supply for **1 year** from the date of service.
- Customer-supplied parts and devices (for example, thermostats bought by the customer) are not covered by our parts guarantee. The 2-year labor warranty still applies to our installation work.
- Refrigerant added during a repair is not covered by any parts warranty.

## 3. New system installations: 10-Year Labor Guarantee

Systems installed by Cedar Ridge include a **10-Year Labor Guarantee**, provided that:

1. The equipment is registered with the manufacturer (Cedar Ridge registers it on your behalf within 30 days of installation), and
2. The system receives professional maintenance at least once every 12 months, with proof of service available on request.

If maintenance lapses for more than 18 months, the labor guarantee reverts to 2 years from the installation date.

## 4. What is not covered

- Damage from flooding, fire, lightning, power surges, freezing, pests or other acts of nature
- Repairs or modifications by anyone other than Cedar Ridge
- Damage caused by lack of maintenance, dirty filters or blocked airflow
- Equipment moved from its original installation location
- Improper fuel, voltage or use
- Routine maintenance items such as filters, belts and humidifier pads
- Cosmetic damage and normal wear

## 5. Making a warranty claim

Call (555) 014-2200 with your invoice number. If the problem is covered, there is no charge for labor and the diagnostic fee is waived. If the problem is not covered, the standard diagnostic fee applies and you will receive a price before any work begins.

Warranty coverage can only be confirmed by our office after reviewing your service records and equipment information.

## 6. Transfer

The 10-Year Labor Guarantee on installations can be transferred once to a new homeowner. The transfer request must be made within 60 days of the home sale and includes a $50 transfer fee. Repair labor warranties are not transferable.
