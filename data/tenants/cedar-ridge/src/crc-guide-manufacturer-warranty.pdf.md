# Understanding Manufacturer Equipment Warranties

_Customer guide · Effective January 1, 2026 · Fictional company for ServiceIQ testing_

Your HVAC equipment has two separate kinds of warranty coverage:

| Coverage | Provided by | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| Parts warranty | The equipment manufacturer | The cost of replacement parts |
| Labor warranty | The installing or repairing contractor (Cedar Ridge) | The labor to diagnose and replace parts |

## Typical manufacturer parts warranties

- **10-year parts warranty** when the equipment is registered with the manufacturer within 60 days of installation.
- **5-year parts warranty** if the equipment is not registered in time.
- Some product lines offer longer coverage on major components such as compressors or heat exchangers.

Exact terms depend on the manufacturer, model, serial number and installation date. Cedar Ridge registers equipment we install within 30 days, so customers receive the longer registered coverage.

## What manufacturer warranties usually do not cover

- Labor (covered separately by your contractor's labor warranty)
- Refrigerant
- Equipment installed by unlicensed contractors
- Damage from improper installation, lack of maintenance or acts of nature

## Checking your coverage

To check coverage on equipment you already own, provide our office with the model and serial number from the equipment's data plate, or your Cedar Ridge invoice number. **We cannot confirm coverage for a specific unit without looking it up.**
