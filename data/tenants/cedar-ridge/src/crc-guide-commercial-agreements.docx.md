# Commercial Preventive Maintenance Agreements

_Effective January 1, 2026 · Fictional company for ServiceIQ testing_

## Who it's for

Owners and managers of light commercial properties with rooftop units or split systems up to 25 tons.

## Agreement levels

| Level | Visits per year | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Essential | 2 (spring and fall) | Inspection, cleaning, belt check, filter change (filters billed at cost) |
| Plus | 4 (quarterly) | Essential tasks plus coil cleaning and quarterly filter changes (filters included) |

## Benefits

- 4-hour emergency response target during standard hours for agreement customers
- 10% off repair labor
- Equipment condition reports after every visit

## Pricing

Agreements are priced per site based on the number, size and age of units. Request a site survey to receive a quote. Commercial service is not available in Riverton (45869) or Wapello Crossing (45895).
