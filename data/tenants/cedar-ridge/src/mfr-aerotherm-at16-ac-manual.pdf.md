## Safety

- This unit contains a mildly flammable (A2L) refrigerant. Only certified technicians may service the refrigerant circuit.
- Disconnect all power before cleaning around the unit.
- Do not operate the unit with panels removed.

## Homeowner maintenance

- Keep at least 24 inches (2 feet) of clearance around the outdoor unit and 5 feet above it.
- Remove leaves, grass clippings and debris from the coil fins. You may gently rinse the coil with a garden hose from the inside out after disconnecting power. Do not use a pressure washer.
- Replace or clean the indoor air filter regularly.
- Have the system professionally inspected once a year.

## Normal operation

- The outdoor fan may continue to run briefly after the compressor stops.
- The unit has a built-in 5-minute anti-short-cycle delay. After a power interruption or thermostat change, the compressor may wait up to 5 minutes before starting. This is normal.

## Before calling for service

- Confirm the thermostat is set to Cool and below room temperature.
- Check the outdoor disconnect and the circuit breakers.
- Check the indoor air filter.
- If the unit still does not run, contact your Aerotherm dealer.

## Warranty

10-year limited parts warranty when registered within 60 days of installation; 5-year limited parts warranty if not registered. Labor is not covered by the manufacturer.
