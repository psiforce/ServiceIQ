# Emergency Safety Guide

_Version 3 · Effective September 1, 2026 · Approved by Cedar Ridge Safety Manager · Fictional company for ServiceIQ testing_

This guide tells customers what to do first in an emergency. In every life-threatening situation, call 911 first.

## If you smell gas (rotten-egg smell)

1. Leave the home immediately with all people and pets.
2. Do not turn lights, appliances or the thermostat on or off. Do not use phones, light matches or start vehicles in an attached garage.
3. Leave doors open as you exit if it is safe to do so.
4. From a safe distance outside, call your gas utility's emergency number or 911.
5. Do not re-enter until the gas utility or fire department says it is safe.
6. Call Cedar Ridge only after the home has been made safe. We do not respond to active gas leaks.

## If your carbon monoxide (CO) alarm sounds

1. Get everyone outside into fresh air immediately.
2. Call 911.
3. Do not re-enter until emergency responders say it is safe.
4. Call our emergency line at (555) 014-2299 to inspect fuel-burning heating equipment before using it again.

## Burning smell, smoke or sparks from HVAC equipment

1. Turn the system off at the thermostat.
2. If it is safe, switch off the furnace power switch or the breaker for the equipment.
3. If you see smoke or flames, leave the home and call 911.
4. If there is no smoke or fire, call (555) 014-2299.

## Water leaking from equipment

1. Turn the system off at the thermostat.
2. Do not touch electrical components or wiring that is wet.
3. Move belongings away and contain the water with towels or a bucket.
4. Call (555) 014-2299.

## No heat in freezing weather

If outdoor temperatures are below 40°F and your heat is out, call (555) 014-2299. Never use an oven, gas stove or outdoor heater to heat the home. Use space heaters only according to the manufacturer's instructions and keep them away from anything flammable.
