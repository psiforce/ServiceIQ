# Aerotherm AG96 Series Gas Furnace: Homeowner's Manual (Excerpt)

_Aerotherm is a fictional manufacturer created for ServiceIQ testing · Document AG96-HO-2025 rev C_

## Models covered

AG96V060, AG96V080, AG96V100 and AG96V120 two-stage, variable-speed gas furnaces (96% AFUE). The last three digits indicate input capacity in thousands of BTU per hour.

## Safety warnings

- **If you smell gas:** do not try to light any appliance, do not touch any electrical switch, and do not use any phone in the building. Leave the building and call your gas supplier from a neighbor's phone or a safe location. If you cannot reach your gas supplier, call the fire department.
- Install carbon monoxide alarms as required by local code.
- Do not store gasoline or flammable materials near the furnace.
- Do not operate the furnace with the blower door removed.

## Status light (LED) codes

The control board has a status LED visible through the blower door window. Count the flashes.

| LED signal | Meaning | Homeowner action |
|---|---|---|
| Steady on | Normal operation (power on) | None |
| Off | No power to the furnace | Check the furnace switch and breaker |
| 1 flash | Flame lockout (failed to ignite after 3 tries) | Turn power off for 30 seconds and back on once. If it repeats, call for service. |
| 2 flashes | Pressure switch stuck closed | Call for service |
| 3 flashes | Pressure switch did not close (possible blocked vent or drain) | Check that outdoor vent pipes are not blocked by snow or debris, then call for service |
| 4 flashes | High-limit switch open (overheating) | Replace a dirty air filter and open supply vents. If it repeats, call for service. |
| 5 flashes | Flame sensed with gas valve closed | Turn the furnace off and call for service immediately |
| 6 flashes | Reversed polarity or poor ground | Call for service |
| Rapid flash | Low flame signal (dirty flame sensor) | Call for service |

## Homeowner maintenance

- Inspect and replace the air filter regularly.
- Keep outdoor intake and exhaust vent pipes clear of snow, ice and debris.
- Have the furnace inspected annually by a qualified technician.

## Warranty

10-year limited parts warranty when registered within 60 days of installation (5 years if not registered). Lifetime limited heat exchanger warranty for the original owner when registered. Labor is not covered by the manufacturer.
