# Financing Terms

_Effective January 1, 2026 · Lending partner: Keystone Home Finance (fictional) · Fictional company for ServiceIQ testing_

Financing is offered by Keystone Home Finance, not by Cedar Ridge. All financing is subject to credit approval by the lender.

| Plan | APR | Term | Minimum purchase |
|---|---|---|---|
| Promotional deferred interest | 0% if paid in full within 18 months | 18 months | $3,000 |
| Fixed-rate installment | 9.99% | Up to 120 months | $3,000 |

## Important details

- **Deferred interest:** On the 0% plan, if the balance is not paid in full within 18 months, interest is charged from the purchase date on the original amount at the lender's standard rate.
- Financing applies to new equipment installations only. Repairs, maintenance and Comfort Club memberships cannot be financed.
- Applications take about 10 minutes and can be completed with your comfort advisor or online.
- Cedar Ridge cannot guarantee approval or the rate you will receive.
- Questions about statements or payments should be directed to the lender.
